I built an AI image generation pipeline using Stable Diffusion XL to test how different prompts, settings and techniques affect the quality of generated images.
First, I tested different prompt styles from simple descriptions to more detailed ones to see which produced the best results. I also compared two schedulers, DPM++ 2M and Euler and used CLIP scores to measure how closely the generated images matched their prompts.
Next I used ControlNet with Canny edge detection. This allowed me to upload my own product images and generate new versions while trying to keep the original product's shape and structure. I tested different conditioning scales to see how much control the original image had over the final result.
I also compared different model configurations and used CLIP scores and LPIPS to evaluate the results. These measurements helped me understand how well the images matched the prompts and how visually different they were.
This project helped me understand how AI image generation works and how changing prompts, models and settings can affect the final images. I also explored how these techniques could be used in e-commerce to create professional product images while keeping products recognisable and accurate.
