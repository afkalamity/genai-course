I built a convolutional VAE using FashionMNIST and created a 4×4 grid of generated images along with a latent-space grid to see how changing two dimensions affected the results. I also trained three different GAN models using the same dataset. The first was a standard GAN, the second had a much stronger discriminator and the third used WGAN-GP. I compared all three using FID scores on 500 images as well as sample grids and training loss graphs. One thing that surprised me was that the second GAN did not produce just one type of image like I expected. It still generated different items such as shirts, sneakers and dresses but the images were quite noisy and had small dots and patterns. WGAN-GP also did not look as good as I expected. It generated a wider variety of images but they looked blurry and had strange stripe patterns. From this experiment I learned that making a model more stable during training does not always mean it will produce better images. Even if a model avoids generating the same image over and over again the overall quality can still be poor.
