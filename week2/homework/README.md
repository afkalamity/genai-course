I built a DDPM using FashionMNIST and tested three different noise schedules: linear, cosine and modified cosine. I compared their results using image grids and FID scores. I also changed the model so it could generate specific types of clothing like T-shirts and sneakers by using class labels. I tested different guidance values (1, 3, 7 and 15) to see how they affected the images. What surprised me was that the results changed depending on how long the model trained. At epoch 20 the linear schedule worked best with an FID of 47 but at epoch 40 the cosine schedule performed better with an FID of 36 compared to 42 for linear. The modified cosine schedule actually got worse with more training as its FID went from 86 to 150. For the guidance test I noticed that higher guidance made the images clearer and helped them match the requested class better but when the value was too high the images started looking distorted and less realistic.
